Help & FAQs

What is the move-out policy?

You must provide a 30-day notice via the app. Security deposit is settled after room inspection.

How are meals auto-paused on leave?

Once you declare leave dates, the system automatically opts you out of meals for those days. You don't need to toggle anything manually.

Can guests visit my room?

Guests are only allowed in the lobby area during daytime hours.

How do I get a receipt for HRA?

Go to Payments → History → tap any payment → Download Receipt. If your PG has enabled HRA statements, you'll also see a "Generate HRA Statement" option.
